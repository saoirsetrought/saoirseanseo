My favourite day of the week is Friday. 

I sit down and gather all pieces of tech media that I consumed that week, try to make sense of it and then put three of the biggest stories in a more consumable video. I’ve been doing that for just over a year now, which is crazy to think.

I actually enjoy watching/reading the news and when I was younger, I went through a phase of wanting to be an RTÉ news reader? It was tradition to come home from school and eat dinner at my grandparents’ house before plonking down in front of the telly with Grandad to watch The Six O’Clock news.

RTÉ never came knocking so I decided to fulfil my own news reporting dreams. Last year, a friend asked me how I keep on top of the pace of the tech industry. I rattled off a long list of resources to help her:

- Tech Crunch
- Reuters

- The Irish Times
- The Wall Street Journal

- Silicon Republic
- The Financial Times

etc etc…

But how do we know what’s important and what’s crap? Not to mention the paid subscriptions for some of these platforms… SO I decided to take one for the team!

Now every Friday (*sometimes* a Saturday or Sunday), I post a video on the tech news you NEED to know - if you’re in the tech industry or otherwise - because it’s stuff that actually **impacts** you.

Very proud that it’s become a day that others look forward too:)
