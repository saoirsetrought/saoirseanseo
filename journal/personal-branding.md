On the 12th of November, saoirse went to her first LinkedIn workshop.

I’ve been thinking a lot about increasing your surface area of luck and building a personal brand is one of the most accessible ways to do that.

I’ve never put this in writing before but I did get my current job as a result of having a personal brand. Making the decision to pivot away from a “technical” role - in my case, data science - to a job in marketing was a choice driven by my eagerness to show others what can be done with technology.

Everyone thought I was mad. I had no traditional experience in marketing and all I’d ever known was building so HOW was I to stand out in a sea of applications of people who were (very probably) more qualified than me.

*It was then that saoirse made the bold move to include her TikTok on her CV.*

Again, people thought I was mad. The concept of posting on social media is looked down on in Ireland and here I was, putting it on my CV. I’d been making tech videos for around 6 months at that stage - how to build cool web apps, optimising your Notion, Claude tricks, career advice. And THAT is what I thought would make me stand out.

I will admit, I didn’t think about it too much when I started posting - I was just doing it for the love of creating. But the material resonated with people, which gave me the confidence to reach out to the hiring manager on LinkedIn and send over my CV.

All of that to say → don’t be afraid to put yourself out there. If it’s not in your nature to do it, then be uncomfortable. If you think it’s cringe, climb cringe mountain.

You’re your own biggest blocker.
